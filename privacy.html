RyftTiers

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what the RyftTiers Discord bot and the RyftTiers website (ryfttiers.pages.dev) collect, why, and what you can do about it. RyftTiers is a free, community-run Minecraft PvP tier project. We don’t sell your data or use it for advertising.

1. What we collect

  • Discord info: your Discord user ID, username and server display name, and the RyftTiers roles you have.
  • Minecraft info: the Minecraft username you verify with, its UUID, and which platform you play on (Java, Bedrock or cracked).
  • Testing data: your region, the queues you join, your tiers per gamemode, test results and history, who tested you, and test cooldowns.
  • Messages you send to the bot’s features: messages in test and support ticket channels (so they can be shown in the website’s chat and ticket views), and the answers you give in tester applications.
  • Commands: the slash commands, buttons and ! commands you use, and your coin balance.
  • Voice activity: whether you’re in voice channels used for tests. We don’t record or listen to audio.
  • Website sign-in: if you sign in with Discord on the website, we link that login to your Discord ID and player profile.

The bot reads messages in the RyftTiers server only to respond to commands and run tickets and tests. It doesn’t store general chat.

2. How we use it

  • To run test queues and tests, record results, and assign tier and verification roles.
  • To show public tiers, stats and leaderboards on the website and through the public RyftTiers API.
  • To handle support tickets and tester applications.
  • To moderate and prevent cheating or abuse, for example by checking that a Minecraft account is real with Mojang’s API.

3. What is public

Your Minecraft username, UUID, region, tiers and test history are shown publicly on the website and in the public API, since that is the purpose of a tier list. Ticket messages and tester applications are only visible to you and RyftTiers staff.

4. Where it’s stored and who we share it with

Data is stored in a database hosted by Supabase. The bot runs on Render, and the website on Cloudflare Pages. We look up Minecraft usernames with Mojang’s public API. We don’t share your data with anyone else, except where required by law. Discord’s own handling of your data is covered by Discord’s Privacy Policy.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your player profile, tiers and test history while RyftTiers is running, so the tier list stays accurate. Queue entries and live test data are removed once the test ends. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

6. Your choices

You can stop using RyftTiers at any time. To see, correct or delete your data, open a support ticket in the RyftTiers Discord server. If we delete your data, your tiers and history are removed and you’ll need to verify and be tested again to get them back.

7. Children

RyftTiers is meant for people who meet Discord’s minimum age. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Also see our Terms of Service.
